# Product overview

## Purpose

PrepPulse is a physician assistance workspace for reviewing extensive patient histories. It reduces the time required to locate important information across dated laboratory reports, consultations, admissions, prescriptions and other records. It organizes the available history into a readable consultation view while keeping each conclusion connected to its source.

The product is designed to support record review. It is not an autonomous diagnostic or treatment system. A clinician remains responsible for interpreting the record, confirming findings and approving any care plan.

## User roles

Administrators register patients, create and manage accounts, assign doctors and caregivers, and schedule appointments. Doctors review assigned patients, analyze records, read the source-linked brief, ask questions, browse prior visits, and prepare signed care pathways. Caregivers access only the signed plans assigned to them, report checklist completion and submit observations for the doctor.

## Patient history as the organizing unit

Each patient has a permanent PrepPulse identity. Existing data aliases are searchable, and visits, appointments, source files, conversations, charts, decisions and signed care plans remain attached to that patient. New document uploads append to the patient's history rather than creating a one-time consultation record.

## Clinical boundary

Generated summaries and workflow suggestions are assistive. Absence from uploaded records is not proof that a test or event never occurred. Unsupported values are not estimated to fill gaps. A source preview is available for clinician verification, and plan content is not sent to a caregiver until a clinician reviews and signs it.
